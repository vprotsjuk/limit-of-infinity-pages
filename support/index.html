How to play

  1. Choose a mode: Limit (levels) or Infinity (an endless flight to watch).
  2. Touch the screen and pull the shape back, like a slingshot. A thin line shows where it will go.
  3. Let go. The shape flies at constant speed and bounces off the walls with true mirror reflections.
  4. In Limit, hit the glowing wall, then reach the hole. Shapes stop when they rest in a corner, and shapes of the same kind stick together.
  5. In Infinity, just watch: the flight never has to end, and the music follows it.
  6. Every flight can be kept as a replay and shared as a picture.

FAQ

I hear no sound.

The game respects the iPhone silent switch. Flip the switch on the side of the phone so the orange mark is hidden, and raise the volume. Sound starts with your first touch. The in-game Sound tab has its own volumes for hits and background.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device: levels, stars, settings and kept replays. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account, so progress does not move to another device by itself. Deleting the app deletes its progress.

Does it need internet?

No. The whole game works offline.

Does it collect any data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

The phone gets warm.

The starry sky and sound run continuously. Low Power Mode makes the game gentler on the battery.
