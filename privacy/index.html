Privacy Policy

Limit of Infinity · Effective date: October 17, 2026

In short

Limit of Infinity does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, identifiers or usage statistics.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or any other third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not need an internet connection and does not send anything to any server.

What is stored on your device

To remember your game, the app keeps a small amount of data only on your iPhone: your settings (such as sound and look), your level progress and stars, and the replays you choose to keep. This is stored in the app's own storage on the device, including the storage iOS provides for apps. It never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud or iTunes backups for your device, Apple may include this app data in your device backup under your own Apple account; the developer has no access to it.

Sharing

If you choose to share a replay or picture, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is your decision; the app itself transmits nothing.

Third parties

None. No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads. The only link that leaves the app is «Write to us», which opens your own mail app addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that involved collecting data would first require an app update that you could choose not to install.
